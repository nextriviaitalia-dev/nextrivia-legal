The game

NexTrivia is a quiz game with verified questions in six languages: Duels and Arenas from 2 to 6 players, and private rooms by invitation. The server decides timings, valid answers and scores: where the device and the server disagree, the server wins.

Questions never cover politics, religion or geopolitics.

Sparks

Sparks are an in-game virtual currency that gives access to matches and to cosmetics such as avatars and frames.

  • They are not a wager. Entering a match puts nothing at stake: no prize pool, no cash winnings, no prize draw.
  • They cannot be converted into money and cannot be transferred between accounts.
  • They do not buy competitive advantage: cosmetics and entries, never answers, extra time or points.
  • There is nothing paid and random: no loot boxes, no surprises. What you buy is what you saw before buying.
  • Purchased Sparks do not expire for as long as the account exists.

Purchases go through Apple, which is the only party that takes payment. Refunds follow Apple's rules; when Apple tells us about a refund, the corresponding Sparks are taken back from the balance, which may go negative until the next credit.
