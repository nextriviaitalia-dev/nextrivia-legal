Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

NexTrivia is a competitive quiz game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what data we process, why, on what legal basis and for how long. It is written under Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

Data controller

Riccardo Fatone, Via Entratico 4, 00188 Rome (RM), Italy.

For anything concerning personal data: nextriviaitalia@gmail.com.

We have not appointed a data protection officer: none of the cases in Article 37 GDPR apply.

What we process

Guest account. The first time you open the app the server creates a random identifier and a device token. Playing requires no name, e-mail, age or phone number.

Profile. The display name you choose, the avatar (symbol and colours, or a photo you decide to upload), the equipped title and frame, your country if you set one by hand, and the three trophies in your showcase.

Sign in with Apple or with e-mail (optional). Your Apple user identifier and, if you choose to share it, your e-mail address; or an e-mail and a password, stored only as a hash. They exist so you can find your profile again on another device.

Gameplay. Matches played, answers, scores, statistics, achievements, weekly goals, season progress, leaderboards, friends, requests and invites.

Sparks. The movements of the in-game wallet: daily gifts, goals, match entries, in-app purchases verified by Apple. We never receive payment details: transactions are handled entirely by Apple, which only tells us that a purchase happened.

Reports and moderation. If you report a player: who reported, who was reported, the reason, the optional text you write, the room and match involved, and the actions a moderator took.

Technical. Device language, app version, IP address while connected. The IP is used to make the connection work and to rate-limit abuse, and is not kept beyond the server logs.

What we do not process. The game has no written chat: the only thing a player can send to a room is one of six preset emoji. We do not collect location, address book, contacts, health data, advertising identifiers or biometrics.

Why, and on what legal basis

PurposeLegal basis
Running the online game: rooms, matches, scores, leaderboards, friends, SparksPerformance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — these are the services you ask for by using the app
Preventing abuse, cheating and offensive names; handling reports, blocks and sanctionsLegitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) in player safety and the integrity of the game
Keeping the record of Sparks movementsLegal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) and legitimate interest in accurate accounting
Answering what you write to nextriviaitalia@gmail.comPerformance of a contract and legitimate interests

Providing profile data is necessary to play online. You can use the app in practice mode with no account and no connection.

What we do not do

No advertising. No tracking for advertising purposes and no advertising identifier. No sale or sharing of data. No third-party analytics SDK inside the app. No automated decision-making producing legal effects on you under Article 22 GDPR: sanctions for abuse are decided by a person.

Who else sees the data

  • Apple, as the provider of Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases and push notifications, for the parts that concern it and under its own policy.
  • Our hosting provider, which runs our server as a processor appointed under Article 28 GDPR.
  • Other players see only what your public profile exposes: name, avatar, frame, title, level, showcased trophies and — only if you set it and choose to make it visible — your country. They never see your e-mail, age, location, reports or moderation history.

Where the data is

Our servers are in the European Economic Area. Should we ever use a provider outside the EEA, the transfer will happen only under an adequacy decision or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, and this policy will be updated first.

How long we keep it

  • Profile, statistics and progress: as long as the account exists. Delete the account and they are erased immediately.
  • Reports and moderation cases: 12 months from the closing of the case, so appeals and repeat offences can be handled.
  • Sparks movements: kept in anonymised form — detached from your identifier — even after account deletion, for accounting obligations and the integrity of the leaderboards.
  • Server technical logs: 30 days at most.

Your rights

You may exercise at any time the rights in Articles 15–22 GDPR: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection to processing based on legitimate interests.

Two of them are in the app itself, with no need to write to us. Under Settings → Privacy and data:

  • Export my data produces a JSON file with profile, account, statistics, Sparks movements, matches, friends and goals;
  • Delete account immediately erases profile, statistics, friends, avatar (photo included) and the link with Apple.

For everything else write to nextriviaitalia@gmail.com: we answer within one month, as Article 12 GDPR requires.

If you believe the processing infringes the GDPR you may lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, Piazza Venezia 11, 00187 Rome — garanteprivacy.it) or with the supervisory authority of the country you live in.

Children

NexTrivia is rated 13+ and is not intended for children under 13. We do not ask for age and do not knowingly process data of children under 13. If we find that an account belongs to a child below that age we delete it. If you are a parent and believe this has happened, write to nextriviaitalia@gmail.com and we will take care of it.

Changes

If we change this policy we update the date at the top and, when the change is substantial, we say so in the app before it takes effect.
